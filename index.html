    ABOUT THIS EXPERIMENT

    Craft meets curiosity.

    This is a working front-end prototype: guided steps, a rotatable illustrative 3D model, a browser camera, optional hand landmark detection, and local progress.

    Not yet supported: automatic yarn/hook tracking, stitch correctness classification, automatic stitch counting, or physics-accurate crochet. Hand detection only evaluates whether hands are visible and approximately positioned.

    The tutorial is linked to simplydaisy's original video, not copied or rehosted.
